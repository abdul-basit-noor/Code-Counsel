Institutional integrity & governance

A consultancy held to the same standard it advises on

Code Counsel (SMC-Private) Limited operates under the Companies Act, 2017. That governs how we're structured — and it's also the framework most of our clients are trying to navigate.

Who we work with

Startups, enterprises, and institutions

We work with founders incorporating a company for the first time, enterprises trying to clean up a compliance backlog, and institutions — including corporate bodies, government offices, and international donors — that need advisory work delivered to a professional standard rather than informally.

Our founding focus is compliance automation: helping national startups navigate SECP and FBR requirements without needing a full-time legal or finance hire before they can afford one. Where a client's needs run into web development, digital positioning, or corporate legal drafting, that work is handled in-house under our other two registered classifications, rather than handed off to a third party.

How we work

Principles that shape every engagement

Transparency in scope

Every engagement is set out in writing before work begins — classification, deliverables, timeline, and fee — so there's nothing to renegotiate midway through.

Right-sized advice

We size recommendations to what a client can realistically implement and maintain, not to what looks most sophisticated on paper.

In-house delivery

Legal, technical, and strategic work is carried out by our own team, so the same people who scope an engagement are accountable for delivering it.

Filed, not just advised

Where a deliverable needs to be lodged with SECP, FBR, or another authority, we see it through to filing rather than stopping at a recommendation.
