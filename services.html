Registered practice classifications

What Code Counsel is licensed and equipped to do

Every engagement sits under one of three classifications registered with SECP. That keeps scope, fees, and deliverables specific — never a generic "consulting retainer."

Class 35

Strategic consultancy

For organisations that need outside judgment on how to plan, run, or scale a programme — not just paperwork filed on their behalf.

Strategic planning & advisory

Working sessions and written recommendations on organisational strategy, market entry, or programme design.

Project management & monitoring

Structuring and tracking delivery for time-bound projects, including donor-funded and government-linked work.

Institutional capacity building

Process documentation, governance structuring, and training so an institution's own team can maintain what we help set up.

Class 42

Technology & digital

Development and infrastructure work carried out by an in-house technical team, not outsourced and re-billed.

Software & web architecture

Websites, internal tools, and web applications built to a specification, with source handed over on completion.

Cloud solutions & automation

Hosting setup, workflow automation, and integration work that reduces manual, repeatable admin.

Digital branding & SEO strategy

Positioning, on-page SEO, and content structuring aimed at organic visibility rather than one-off traffic spikes.

Class 45

Legal advisory

Regulatory and corporate legal support scoped to Pakistani law, delivered in writing and filed where required.

Corporate & business legal advisory

Incorporation documents, shareholder agreements, and day-to-day corporate legal questions.

Regulatory compliance governance

SECP and FBR compliance mapping, filing calendars, and remediation where a company has fallen behind.

Ancillary corporate support

Drafting and review of contracts, notices, and internal policies that sit alongside a company's core filings.
